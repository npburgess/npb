## About

Bespoke Betty makes handmade, made-to-order shirts, hoodies, jumpers and
cushions. The site is a **portfolio, not a shop** — a single page that shows the
work and opens a conversation through a contact form. The design direction,
"Thread", is warm and tactile: Fraunces paired with Inter, a clay accent used
with restraint, and dashed "stitch line" seams tying the sections together.

## Notable

- **Betty edits it herself.** The gallery and homepage copy are content-managed
  through **Sveltia CMS** at `/admin` (GitHub login), so new pieces and text
  changes don't need a developer.
- **No backend to run.** The contact form uses **Netlify Forms** — submissions
  are captured with a honeypot for spam and the visitor lands on a thank-you
  page, all with zero server code.
- **Rebrandable from tokens.** Colour, type and spacing live as design tokens in
  one stylesheet, so the whole look can shift without touching markup.
- Statically built and hosted on Netlify, live on its own domain with
  auto-issued TLS.

## Stack

Astro static build, deployed to Netlify with Netlify Forms for contact and
Sveltia CMS for client-side editing. Self-hosted Fraunces + Inter via
Fontsource. Live at [bespokebetty.com.au](https://bespokebetty.com.au).
