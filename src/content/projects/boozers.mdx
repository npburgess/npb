## About

Boozers Squash is a fun little site for finding somewhere to play — a place to
have a hit of squash, meet new people, and make some friends over a few beers
along the way. The friendly front door to a Launceston club.

## Notable

- Static single page built with **Astro**, deployed on **Netlify**.
- Design tokens and shared styles in one global stylesheet — simple to maintain.

## Stack

Astro, deployed on Netlify.
