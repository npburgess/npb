## About

Boozers Squash is a squash club at the Theogenes Complex in Launceston, Tasmania. It
started as a single-page marketing site, the friendly front door for finding somewhere
to play, having a hit, and meeting people over a few beers afterwards. It has since grown
a member-only app that runs the club's pennant nights end to end.

The public page is still the shopfront. Behind it, at `/app`, sits a portal members reach
on their phones: sign in, score matches live, and check where their team sits.

## The member app

Members sign in with a name and a PIN and land on the tools they actually use on a
pennant night:

- **Live scoring** with two big tap panels, built for a phone at courtside. The scoring
  engine is a pure, unit-tested module: point-a-rally to 15, win-by-2 from 14 all,
  best-of-five, with serve-side tracking and an **Undo** that rewinds a single point
  cleanly, even back across a game boundary.
- **Results and ladders** that recompute themselves. Standings and per-player stats are
  database views, so every table stays consistent with whatever has been recorded or
  corrected.
- **Rosters, teams, and stats** for the current pennant, plus a **player directory** of
  active members.
- **Fill-ins with no paperwork.** Short a player? Any member can add a guest on the fly and
  score against them. The result counts on the ladder for the member opponent while the
  guest stays profile-free.

## The admin console

Admins get an extra tab set, gated by row-level security so the write paths simply do not
exist for a normal member:

- **Generate a pennant.** Name it, list the teams, order the roster by strength, and build.
  A snake draft balances the sides so each team's top line is a genuine top seed, and a
  round-robin schedule lays out the rounds and line-versus-line fixtures. The pennant is
  built as a draft and only flips to active once every step succeeds, so a failed build
  rolls itself back.
- **Manage the season.** Edit rosters and games, move players between teams and lines,
  adjust seed ranks and contact details, and toggle active members versus fill-ins.
  Deactivating a departed player drops them from the sign-in list while keeping their
  match history intact.
- **Finals and rankings.** Generate the finals series and organise the rankings that seed
  it.
- **Corrections.** Void and re-score a completed result, reassign a side to another member
  or a guest, and fix a round date. Because the stats are views, everything downstream
  recomputes on its own.

## Architecture

The whole thing stays a static deploy. The app is a client-only React island embedded in
the Astro site, talking straight to **Supabase** (Postgres, Auth, and row-level security)
with no backend server of its own. Netlify serves plain static files; Supabase enforces
who can read and write. Authentication is PIN-based, so the club never has to send members
email, and the entire stack is owned under a club account so it can be handed over intact.

## Stack

Astro 5 and a React 19 island, TypeScript throughout, Supabase for data and auth, deployed
static on Netlify. The public site keeps its `LocalBusiness` structured data and cookieless
analytics; the scoring and draft logic live in pure modules with unit tests.
