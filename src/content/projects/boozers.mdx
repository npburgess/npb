## About

Boozers Squash is a single-page marketing site for a squash club at the Theogenes
Complex in Launceston. Membership and court bookings are handled off-site by
Launceston Squash — this site is the front door.

## Notable

- Static single page built with **Astro**, deployed on **Netlify**.
- Design tokens and shared styles in one global stylesheet — simple to maintain.

> A proof-of-concept build: some imagery is placeholder illustration, but the
> copy and details are real.

## Stack

Astro, deployed on Netlify.
